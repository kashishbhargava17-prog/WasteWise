02 · GUIDE

Learn the basics

Simple examples help you understand common waste categories.

🍃

Wet Waste

Food and other biodegradable household waste.

Examples: food scraps, fruit peels
📦

Dry / Recyclable

Clean, dry materials that may be recyclable.

Examples: paper, cardboard
🧴

Plastic

Plastic packaging and containers.

Examples: bottles, containers
🔋

E-waste

Discarded electronic or battery-powered items.

Examples: batteries, old chargers
⚠️

Special Waste

Items that need separate handling or local guidance.

Always follow local disposal rules.

Note: Waste categories and collection rules can differ by location. WasteWise is an educational prototype; follow your local waste authority's instructions for actual disposal.
